# Matt Soucoup’s blog

The homepage is for reading. A compact plum wordmark links home. Lead straight into the newest article with a dark sans-serif title. Talks & courses share one page; About is separate. Keep each navigation label on one line.

Use a white background, dark ink and blue links. Set essays in Palatino and navigation and headings in Avenir Next, with local fallbacks. Pair each navigation and social link label with a small, decorative inline icon. Keep the text column under 43rem and let navigation wrap on small screens. No banner, oversized type, decorative diagram or motion before the writing.
The default theme follows the reader's system. The footer offers a persistent light or dark override. Individual posts may set `headerSketch` to a decorative image path; omit it for the plain text header.
